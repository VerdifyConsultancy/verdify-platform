# Verdify platform product intent

**Status:** approved

Verdify keeps one 367 sq ft Longmont greenhouse and its live crops safe while turning device control, sensing, telemetry, bounded planning, releases, and crop-care actions into an auditable system. Jason is the greenhouse owner and protected-action gate; authorized agents own bounded implementation and verification; crop and controls reviewers need reproducible evidence; public technical consumers receive read-only evidence without device authority.

## Current software-recovery promise

The product will stop fabricating interior DLI, stop mislabeling configuration drift as reconnect, restore the bounded planner, use center-only VPD mist, reserve south/west mist for intentional irrigation, automate only commissioned wall fertigation, dry the house overnight using solar phase and existing safety rails, and retire the stale June band experiment.

The product does not claim a universal lime-and-cannabis fertilizer recipe. Weekly is a controlled pilot cadence; measured commissioning and conservative shared-line chemistry determine whether actuation is admitted. It also does not add physical sensors or equipment, create a second environment or writer, move deterministic safety into AI, or touch the separate marketing and CRM products.

Success is observed in the live system: no redundant stable-connection bulk writes, no climate mist outside center, no fertilizer outside wall drips, no published interior DLI while invalid, planner triggers resolved through a healthy MCP path, dry-out admitted only at night, and every release tied to exact code, artifact, runtime, and rollback evidence.
