# September 4 hot/dry wetting interruption: controller cause and bounded repair (#778)

This report supersedes the **causal disposition** of the [v2 unresolved report](wetting-incident-2026-09-04.md). It leaves the v1/v2 files and their hashes unchanged. All times below are UTC; Denver was UTC−06:00. The requested window is September 4 20:30 through September 5 00:45 UTC (14:30–18:45 MDT).

## Finding

The device-reported firmware `2026.7.10.1500.09ee886` has a control contradiction: it requests fog with the vent open for hot/dry cooling, then vetoes that fog whenever house temperature reaches **90°F**, five degrees below the 95°F safety ceiling. `vent_mist_assist_active` uses the **sealed-humidification** margin `temp < safety_max − safety_max_seal_margin_f` (95−5); `controls.yaml` uses that flag to exempt fog from the closed-vent interlock. At 90°F the exemption disappears while the vent remains open. Even `SAFETY_COOL`, whose pure relay logic explicitly requests survival fog, cannot pass that interlock because the assist flag is false in safety mode.

The live controller-reported sequence matches both threshold crossings: fog turns off at **20:57:03** (14:57 MDT) as temperature rises through 90°F, and returns at **00:13:03** (18:13 MDT) as temperature falls below 90°F. In the 199 minute-spaced action rows between those transitions, every row reports fog false, vent true, `vent_interlock`; 129 still select `VENT_COOL_FOG_ASSIST` and 70 select `SAFETY_COOL`. The largest action-row gap is 66.29 seconds. The full reported interruption is 3h 16m; the public 15:00–18:00 MDT hourly bins are a three-hour subset. The water meter holds at 600 gal through those bins and advances after fog returns. This establishes a **source-backed controller refusal**, with the caveat that API relay state and meter telemetry do not independently prove every physical actuator instant.

The narrow correction exempts an explicit `resolve_equipment()` fog request in `VENTILATE` or `SAFETY_COOL` from only the vent-close interlock. Fog admission physics and the caller's leak, occupancy, irrigation/fertilizer, clock, soft-budget and hard-volume rails remain in force; sealed mist, thermal relief and fault modes retain the interlock. Center mister admission is unchanged. The correction is source-only here: no OTA, controller setting, cap, database row, or physical proof was changed.

## Guard, plan, occupancy and counter checks

- All 244 diagnostic samples report firmware version `2026.7.10.1500.09ee886`, valid SNTP, and strictly nondecreasing uptime (139,264 to 154,505 seconds). `Task WDT` is the retained reset-reason value, not evidence of an in-window reboot. Thirty-eight equipment-source receipts span connection generations 24, 25, 28 and 29; 21 note source-time gaps and three collector-reported gaps. There are no in-window direct snapshots or native counter samples. The minute action stream is strong controller-state evidence but source receipt coverage is incomplete.
- All 244 selected setpoint snapshots report `safety_max=95`, `safety_max_seal_margin_f=5`, `sw_fog_closes_vent=1`, `mister_water_budget_gal=300`, and `sw_occupancy_inhibit=1`. The mister-today estimate stays 157.3797 gal across all 244 climate samples; the cumulative meter ranges 580–621 gal in the window. The two counters have different scope. There is no captured `mister_daily_volume_max_gal` readback or counter-reset-epoch sample, so this report does not infer a hard ceiling. The fog resumption while mister-today stayed fixed also contradicts a persistent volume ceiling as the immediate veto.
- Fifty-one `system_state` occupancy rows report `empty` from `ha_sensor_sync`; the prior retained pod-log projection also has repeated empty pushes. Those are ingestor/HA observations, not direct occupied-pin readback. At the exact stop the controller reports `vent_interlock`, not its higher-priority leak, occupancy, irrigation, budget or invalid-time vetoes. The same veto repeats at every action sample until recovery. A separate unobserved physical fault cannot be disproved, but is unnecessary to explain the controller refusal.
- The only in-window planner delivery before recovery is `Peak Stress` at 21:01:24, after wetting stopped. Its 21:03 pulse-duration change was confirmed and did not restore fog. The `Decline` delivery at 00:29 also follows recovery. The as-of forecast vintages exist for 21:00, 22:00, 23:00 and 00:00 UTC; they predicted outdoor temperatures 93.8, 93.5, 93.1 and 91.1°F respectively. Forecasts contextualize heat but do not control the identified local interlock.
